This privacy policy explains how [Bizzy Bee legal entity] ("Bizzy Bee," "we," "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit bizzybee.thinkabstrakt.com, contact us, use our service, or talk with one of the AI assistants ("bees") we operate for businesses.

Who we are

Bizzy Bee provides AI employees, called bees, to small service businesses. Bees answer phone calls, texts and website chats, follow up with leads, send appointment reminders, ask for and reply to reviews, send invoices and payment reminders, and draft social media posts for the businesses that hire them. People on our team, called Beekeepers, set up and supervise the bees. We are based in Dallas, Texas.

What this policy covers

This policy covers three groups of people. Our role is different for each.

WhoExamplesOur role
Website visitors and prospectsPeople who visit bizzybee.thinkabstrakt.com, fill out a form, book a call with us or email usWe decide how this information is used, as described in this policy.
Business customersBusinesses that hire bees, and the owners, employees and others who use their accountsWe decide how account and billing information is used, as described in this policy.
End customersPeople who call, text, chat or email with a bee, or get messages from a bee, on behalf of a business customerWe process this information on behalf of the business customer, as its service provider.

Our role for end customers

When a bee talks with end customers, we process their information on behalf of the business customer, as its service provider (sometimes called a "processor"). The business customer decides why and how that information is used, and its own privacy policy applies. We use that information only to provide our service to that business, as described in this policy and in our agreement with the business.

If you're an end customer, the best place to start with questions or requests is the business you were dealing with. If you contact us instead, we'll pass your request to that business and help it respond.

This policy doesn't cover the practices of our business customers, or of third-party websites, platforms or services that we link to or that business customers connect to our service, such as calendars, accounting software, review sites and social media.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact forms and booking requests. Your name, business name, email address, phone number, industry and anything else you tell us when you fill out a form, book a call or write to us.
  • Account and billing information. Names and contact details of account users, business details and billing information. Card payments to us are handled by our payment processor, and we don't store full card numbers.
  • Playbook information. What business customers give us to set up their bees, such as services, prices, hours, policies, FAQs, rules, and the names and contact details of team members who receive hand-offs.
  • Communications with us. Emails, calls, meeting notes and support requests.

Information we process on behalf of business customers

  • Contact details. End customers' names, phone numbers, email addresses and, where the job needs it, property or service addresses.
  • Conversation content. What is said in calls, texts, web chats and emails with a bee, including transcripts and summaries, and anything end customers choose to share.
  • Call recordings. Audio recordings of calls, only when the business customer has turned recording on. When recording is on, callers hear a notice at the start of the call.
  • Appointments and job details. Bookings, reminders, cancellations, quotes, invoices and payment status. Bees send secure payment links from the business's own payment system and do not collect payment card numbers.
  • Reviews and posts. Reviews end customers leave on platforms such as Google, and the replies and posts bees draft.
  • Consent and opt-out records. When and how someone agreed to receive messages, and opt-out requests such as STOP replies.
  • Information from connected tools. Customer lists, calendar entries, CRM records and accounting records that a business customer uploads or connects.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device information. IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, links clicked, the website that referred you, and the date and time of visits. In our app, we also log account activity.
  • Call and message records. Phone numbers, dates, times, durations, delivery status and similar records created when bees make, receive or send calls and messages.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. See "Cookies and analytics" below.

Information from other sources

We may receive information from business customers (for example, a customer list), from services they connect to ours, from our service providers (for example, delivery reports from our text message provider), and from public sources such as published reviews.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies that it needs to work, and analytics tools that help us understand how visitors use it, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors found us. Analytics providers may collect information about your use of our website over time. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work without them.

We don't currently use advertising cookies or pixels that share information with ad networks for targeted advertising. If that changes, we'll update this policy first and offer any opt-out the law requires, including honoring browser opt-out signals such as Global Privacy Control where required.

Our website doesn't currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, because there is no common standard for them.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • provide and operate the service, including answering calls and messages, booking appointments, and sending reminders, follow-ups, review requests, invoices and payment reminders, and drafting posts for business customers;
  • set up and supervise bees, including Beekeeper review of shift logs and conversations, troubleshooting and quality checks for the business customer whose bees they are;
  • show business customers what their bees did, through shift logs, hand-offs and the weekly Honey Report;
  • honor opt-outs, and keep records of consent and opt-outs;
  • respond to inquiries, schedule calls and provide support;
  • bill business customers and manage accounts;
  • send business customers and prospects information about our service, which you can opt out of at any time;
  • protect the security of our service, and prevent and investigate fraud, spam and abuse;
  • understand and improve our website and service, within the limits below; and
  • comply with the law, enforce our terms, and protect the rights and safety of our users, end customers and others.

Limits on how we use end customer information

We use end customer information only to provide our service to the business customer it came from, including supporting, securing, troubleshooting and improving that business's bees, and as required by law. We do not sell end customer information, use it to market our own services to end customers, combine it with information from other business customers to build profiles of people, or use it to train AI models for other businesses.

How bees use AI

  • Bees use AI models from third-party AI providers to understand and respond to calls, texts, chats and emails, to turn speech into text and text into speech, and to draft messages, review replies and posts.
  • To do this, the content of conversations and the relevant parts of a business's playbook are sent to those providers for processing.
  • Our AI providers act as our service providers. We choose providers whose terms for business customers say they won't use the data we send them to train their models. Some providers keep data for a limited time, for example to monitor for abuse, as their terms allow. These protections depend on our providers' terms.
  • We don't use end customer information to train AI models for other businesses.
  • Bee voices are synthetic. We don't use callers' voices to identify them, and we don't create voiceprints.
  • Bees don't make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on people, such as approving or denying credit, insurance, housing or a job. Those decisions stay with the business customer's people.
  • AI can make mistakes. Business customers set rules for what their bees may do, Beekeepers review the bees' work, and bees hand conversations to a person when needed. Bees always identify themselves as AI assistants.

How we share information

  • Service providers. Companies that help us run our business and the service, such as telephone and text message providers, email delivery providers, AI model providers, cloud hosting and data storage providers, payment processors, analytics providers, and customer support and scheduling tools. They may use the information only to provide services to us, under contracts that limit how they can use it.
  • The business customer. Information from conversations with end customers is available to the business customer the bee works for, through shift logs, transcripts, recordings (when enabled), hand-offs and reports.
  • Services a business customer connects. When a business customer connects a calendar, CRM, accounting system, review profile or social media account, we send and receive information as the business directs. Those services' own terms and privacy policies apply.
  • Legal and safety reasons. When we believe in good faith that it's needed to comply with the law or legal process, to protect the rights, property or safety of Bizzy Bee, our users, end customers or others, or to respond to an emergency.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, sale of assets or bankruptcy, information may be transferred to a successor, subject to this policy and our commitments to business customers.
  • With consent. When you ask us to share it or agree that we may.

We do not sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising. Mobile information has the additional protections described in "Text messages" below.

Text messages

This section applies to text messages sent by Bizzy Bee, and by bees on behalf of our business customers.

Who sends messages and why

  • Messages from businesses. Bees send texts on behalf of the business named in each message. These can include replies to your questions, appointment confirmations and reminders, follow-ups on quotes you asked for, review requests, invoices and payment reminders and, if you've agreed to receive them, offers and other marketing messages. The business is the sender of these messages.
  • Messages from Bizzy Bee. If you ask us for a call or a demo, or you use our service, we may text you about your request or your account.

How you agree to receive texts

You may agree to receive texts by texting a business or us, by giving your mobile number when you ask for a quote, appointment or call, or by checking a box or otherwise agreeing on a form. You don't have to agree to receive marketing texts to buy anything.

Messaging terms

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. You'll receive one message confirming that you've opted out. You can also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT, or tell us in your own words.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact the business that texted you, or email us at hello@thinkabstrakt.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We don't share mobile information for marketing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service and message delivery, is permitted. All other categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

When a bee texts on behalf of a business, that business is the sender of the messages, and its records of your number and consent are its own.

How long we keep information

  • Website and prospect information: as long as needed to respond to you and follow up, and no longer than [retention period] after our last contact unless you become a customer.
  • Business customer account information: while the account is open, and afterward as needed for billing, tax, legal and accounting purposes.
  • End customer information: as directed by the business customer and our agreement with it. When a business customer's account closes, we delete its end customer information within [number] days after the export period described in our terms. Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule of up to [number] days.
  • Call recordings: [number] days, unless the business customer chooses a shorter period.
  • Consent and opt-out records: as long as needed to honor opt-outs and to show that messages were sent lawfully, which may be several years.

We may keep information longer where the law requires it, or where it's needed to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements.

How we protect information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. These include limiting access to the people who need it, and using encryption for data in transit and at rest. Some safeguards, such as encryption of stored data, are provided by our hosting and other vendors.

Phone calls and standard text messages travel over telephone carrier networks that we don't control and that aren't encrypted end to end. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

If we learn of a security incident affecting personal information, we'll notify affected people as the law requires. For end customer information, we'll promptly notify the business customer and help it meet its own obligations.

Your choices and rights

Choices

  • Marketing emails: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email from us.
  • Text messages: reply STOP.
  • Cookies: adjust your browser settings.

Privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. A growing number of states, including California and Texas, give residents rights such as:

  • to know or access the personal information a business has about them, and to get a copy in a portable format;
  • to correct inaccurate personal information;
  • to delete personal information;
  • to opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising and certain kinds of profiling; and
  • not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

These laws apply to different businesses and information in different ways, and they include exceptions. We'll respond to requests to access, correct or delete personal information that we hold for our own purposes from people in any US state, subject to the exceptions the law allows, and within the time limits those laws set (generally 45 days, which can be extended in some cases).

How to make a request

Email hello@thinkabstrakt.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We'll need to verify your identity, usually by confirming details we already have, such as your email address or phone number. Where the law allows, you can use an authorized agent, and we may ask for proof of the agent's authority.

End customers: if your request is about information a bee handled for a business, we'll forward it to that business and help it respond, because the business controls that information.

Appeals: if we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or by emailing us with "Privacy appeal" in the subject line. We'll respond within the time the law requires. If you disagree with the outcome of your appeal, you can contact your state attorney general.

California residents: we don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law. We don't disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

Children

Our website and service are meant for businesses and adults. They are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Business accounts are for people 18 and older. If we learn that we've collected personal information from a child under 13 without the consent the law requires, we'll delete it. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at hello@thinkabstrakt.com.

International users

Bizzy Bee is based in the United States, and our service is meant for US businesses. We and our service providers process information in the United States, and some providers may process it in other countries where they operate. If you use our website from outside the US, your information will be transferred to and processed in the US, where privacy laws may differ from those where you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the effective date at the top. If we make material changes, we'll give notice before they take effect, for example by emailing business customers or posting a notice on our website.
