What Miles handles

One job, done all the way.

Replies to new leads within a minute and follows up until they book or say no.

01

Replies within a minute

Website form, Angi, Facebook or a missed call: Miles replies within a minute, day or night, by text or email, and by phone when they asked for a call. He says he's your AI assistant up front.

02

Gets what your estimator needs

What's going on, where the job is and how soon they need help. Then Miles offers two open times and books the visit.

03

Follows up on open quotes

Sent a quote and heard nothing? Miles checks in on the days you pick, like day 1, 3, 7 and 14, until they book or tell you no.

04

Brings back old leads

People who asked about a job months ago and never booked get a short, friendly check-in. Some of them still need the work done.

05

Polite, not pushy

Short messages in your voice, sent during the day. No fake deadlines, no pressure lines, no ten texts in a row.

06

Stops when they say stop

If someone says stop, no thanks or that they hired someone else, Miles stops right away and closes the lead. No more messages unless they reach out.

Rules and hand-offs

Knows when to get a person.

You set the rules. Your Beekeeper writes them into Miles's playbook, and Miles sticks to them.

Hands it to a person when

  • A lead who's ready to sign, or wants to talk price, goes to you with the quote and the whole thread.
  • Questions about financing, insurance or warranties go to the office.
  • Anyone who's upset, or says they've been waiting on you, goes to a person right away.
  • Commercial work and jobs over the size your rules set go to your estimator once Miles has the basics.

Never

  • Never sends another message after someone says stop. Not one.
  • Never contacts people who didn't reach out to you. No bought lists, no cold texts.
  • Never offers a discount or changes a quote. Price talk goes to you.
  • Never sends follow-ups late at night. Only brand-new leads get a reply after hours, because they just asked.

Questions

About Miles.

Don't see yours? Ask a Beekeeper on the call.

Won't all those follow-ups annoy people?

Miles keeps them short, spaced out and on a schedule you approve, during the day. Anyone who says stop or no thanks doesn't hear from him again.

Who will Miles contact?

Only people who reached out to you first: form fills, quote requests, missed calls and past leads who agreed to hear from you. He phones only people who asked for a call, and never texts bought lists or strangers.

Can Miles offer a discount to close the deal?

He can mention an offer you've already approved, like a seasonal deal. Anything beyond that, including haggling, goes to you.

How does Miles know when to stop?

He reads every reply and checks your calendar and job software. When a lead books, signs or says no, the follow-ups stop.
