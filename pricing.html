Questions

Fair
questions.

Don't see yours? Ask a Beekeeper on the call.

What's included in the price?

Setup, the phone numbers and texting your bees need, your Beekeeper, the weekly Honey Report and unlimited conversations. You don't pay per call or per text.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Your Beekeeper builds every playbook and connects your tools as part of the plan.

Can I switch bees or plans later?

Yes. Swap a bee, add one or move to the whole hive whenever you like. Changes start on your next billing date, or sooner if you ask.

How do I cancel?

Send one email. There's no contract and no cancellation fee. We'll hand back your data and point your calls wherever you want them.

What if a bee isn't earning its keep?

Tell your Beekeeper. We'd rather fix the playbook, switch you to a bee that fits, or cancel than have you pay for something that isn't working.

Do you charge for extra calls in a busy month?

No. Plans include unlimited conversations, so a storm week or a busy season costs the same as a quiet one.
