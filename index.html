Questions

Fair
questions.

Don't see yours? Ask a Beekeeper on the call.

Will my customers know they're talking to AI?

Yes. Every bee introduces itself as your business's AI assistant and says so again if anyone asks. Customers mostly care that someone picked up and got it right.

What if a bee doesn't know the answer?

It says so, takes a clear message and hands the conversation to you or your Beekeeper. Bees don't guess on prices, warranties or anything outside your rules.

Do I need new software?

No. Bees work inside what you already use: your phone number, calendar, customer list and invoicing. Your Beekeeper connects it all during setup.

How long does setup take?

Most bees start a trial shift within five business days of the first call. You approve their work during the trial, and most are fully on shift by the end of week two.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month and you can cancel with one email. If a bee isn't earning its keep, we want to hear about it early.

What happens to my customers' information?

We use it to do your work and nothing else. It's never used to train models for other businesses, and you can export or delete it whenever you like.
