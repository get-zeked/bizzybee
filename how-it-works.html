How it works

On shift inabout a week.

No new software to learn and nothing to set up yourself. Your Beekeeper does the work, you sign off on every step, and the bees don't talk to a single customer until you say so.

1

Meet

Day 1 · 20 minutes

You tell us where the work piles up. We look at your calls, leads and invoices with you and suggest the bee that would earn its keep first.

You

  • Join a 20-minute call
  • Share roughly how many calls you miss
  • Pick your first bee or crew

We

  • Run the missed-call math with your numbers
  • Introduce your Beekeeper
  • Send a one-page plan the same day
2

Train

Days 2 – 4

Your Beekeeper writes each bee's playbook: your services, prices, hours, service area, tone and the questions customers always ask.

You

  • Send your price list and FAQs
  • Share calendar and invoicing access
  • Tell us your rules and who gets hand-offs

We

  • Build and test every playbook
  • Set up the phone numbers and texting
  • Connect your calendar, CRM and invoicing
3

Trial shift

Days 5 – 10

Bees draft and you approve. Every reply, booking and invoice waits for your yes, and your Beekeeper tunes the playbook every day from what you change.

You

  • Approve or edit drafts from your phone
  • Flag anything that sounds off
  • About 10 minutes a day

We

  • Review every conversation
  • Fix the playbook the same day
  • Tell you when each bee is ready
4

On shift

From week 2

Bees work on their own, inside your rules. Anything unusual comes to you or your Beekeeper with the whole conversation attached.

You

  • Read the Honey Report on Monday
  • Take the hand-offs that need you
  • Change a rule whenever you like

We

  • Watch the shift log every day
  • Check in with you monthly
  • Keep improving the playbooks

What do you need from me to get started?

About an hour over the first week: a 20-minute call, your price list and FAQs, access to your calendar and invoicing, and a few minutes each day to approve drafts during the trial shift.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Your calls forward to your bee when you want them to, and your customers keep calling the number they already have.

What happens during the trial shift?

Bees draft and you approve. Nothing reaches a customer without your yes, and your Beekeeper adjusts the playbook every day based on what you change.

Who do I call if something goes wrong?

Your Beekeeper, a real person on our team with your account in front of them. You'll have their direct line from day one.

Can I change the rules later?

Any time. Tell your Beekeeper or change it in the hive app, and the bee follows the new rule right away.

Will a bee ever handle something it shouldn't?

Bees work inside the rules you set and hand anything unusual to a person. Every conversation is in the shift log, so you can see exactly what happened.
