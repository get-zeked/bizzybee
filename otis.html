What Otis handles

One job, done all the way.

Sends the invoice the day the job is done and follows up until it's paid.

01

Invoices the same day

When the job is marked done, Otis builds the invoice from the job details and sends it by email and text the same day. Invoices over an amount you set wait for your OK.

02

A secure link to pay

Customers pay by card or bank transfer on your payment processor's secure page. Otis never takes card numbers.

03

Polite reminders

If it's not paid by the due date, Otis follows up on your schedule, like 3, 7 and 14 days after. Short, friendly, link included.

04

Deposits and final bills

Bigger jobs can get a deposit request when they're signed and a final bill when they're done, on the terms you set.

05

Books that stay current

Invoices and payments show up in QuickBooks Online as they happen, matched to the right customer and job. Nobody types them in twice.

06

Answers the easy questions

Did my payment go through? Can you resend that? Otis answers from your books at any hour and sends a receipt the moment a payment lands.

Rules and hand-offs

Knows when to get a person.

You set the rules. Your Beekeeper writes them into Otis's playbook, and Otis sticks to them.

Hands it to a person when

  • Any dispute about a charge goes to the office with the invoice, the job notes and the photos.
  • Requests for a payment plan, a discount or more time go to you to decide.
  • Invoices still unpaid after the last reminder go to your office manager, not to collections.
  • Insurance checks, partial payments and anything else out of the ordinary go to a person.

Never

  • Never takes card or bank numbers by text, email or phone. Customers always pay through the secure link.
  • Never changes an amount, adds a late fee or writes off a balance on his own.
  • Never threatens, shames or mentions collections. Reminders stay friendly.
  • Never sends a reminder on an invoice that's in dispute.

Questions

About Otis.

Don't see yours? Ask a Beekeeper on the call.

Is it safe for customers to pay this way?

They pay on your payment processor's secure page, not by text, email or phone. Otis never sees or stores card numbers.

Will reminders bother my customers?

They're short, friendly and spaced out on the schedule you pick. If a customer pushes back or disputes the bill, the reminders stop and a person takes over.

Does Otis replace my bookkeeper?

No. Otis handles the day-to-day: invoices out, payments in, everything logged. Your bookkeeper still does the books, with less to catch up on.

I already invoice from Jobber. Does that work?

Yes. Otis can send and follow up on the invoices your job software makes, so nothing gets entered twice.
