5. Trial shift

Each new bee starts with a trial shift, usually about a week, as part of onboarding. During the trial shift, the bee drafts texts, emails and replies, and nothing is sent to End Customers until you approve it. Before a bee answers live calls or chats, you and your Beekeeper test it with practice conversations. A bee goes on shift, meaning it starts working on its own within your rules, only after you tell us it's ready.

The trial shift is included in your first month's fee. It is not a free trial unless your Order says so. Approving drafts and practice conversations is your business decision; your approval doesn't mean we've reviewed them for legal compliance.
