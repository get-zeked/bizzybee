Rules and hand-offs

Knows when to get a person.

You set the rules. Your Beekeeper writes them into Penny's playbook, and Penny sticks to them.

Hands it to a person when

  • Posts with a price, discount or offer go to you for a yes before they're published.
  • Comments from an unhappy customer go to a person, never into a public back-and-forth.
  • Photos that show people's faces go to you before they're used.
  • After a bad storm or a hard week in town, Penny checks with you before posting anything promotional.

Never

  • Never publishes a price, discount or offer you haven't approved.
  • Never shows house numbers, license plates or customer names without permission.
  • Never passes off stock photos or AI-made images as your work.
  • Never claims things you can't back up, like being the best in town or a warranty you don't offer.

Questions

About Penny.

Don't see yours? Ask a Beekeeper on the call.

Do I have to approve every post?

Only if you want to. During the trial shift you see everything. After that, posts with a price or an offer still wait for your yes, and the rest go out on schedule.

Where do the photos come from?

Your jobs. Your crew takes before and after photos like they already do, and Penny picks the best ones. No stock photos, and no AI-made pictures of work you didn't do.

Will it sound like us?

Your Beekeeper sets Penny's voice from your past posts, your website and how you talk about the work. You'll see drafts during the trial shift and can change anything.

Does Penny run ads?

No. Penny's job is your regular posts and the monthly email. Paid ads are a different job, and we'd rather say so than do it halfway.
